Eyelash extensions and brow design in Roma Norte

Five distinct extension techniques, with the price, the duration, and who each one actually suits.

Eyelash extensions are not one service — they are five distinct techniques, and choosing the right one matters more than the price. Here is the difference, briefly.

Classic 1x1 ($750) places one extension on each natural lash. It is the most natural look and the lightest — ideal for a first time, or if your natural lashes are healthy and full. Flat ($800) uses a flat-based fiber that wraps the natural lash: it weighs less and bonds better, which makes it work well on fine lashes.

YY ($1,000) uses Y-shaped interlaced fibers that create the feel of greater density without adding weight. Hybrid ($1,100) blends classic and volume for an in-between effect, and it is our most requested set because it looks full without looking artificial. Russian volume ($1,200) is maximum density: fans of several ultra-fine fibers on each natural lash.

Fills (from $450) maintain the work and cost considerably less than a new set. The condition is timing: you need at least 50% retention and you need to come in within 21 days. Past that point it is no longer a fill, it is a new application. If you are carrying work from another salon, we will fill it (from $500) depending on the technique used.

If you would rather not wear extensions, a lash lift ($450) curls your natural lash from the root and includes tint and keratin. It lasts six to eight weeks and needs no maintenance.

For brows, lamination ($450) aligns and sets the hair upward to build shape and visual density. Brow design ($450) adds full shaping mapped to your face. And if you only need maintenance, a brow wax ($220) is the simple service.

We also offer facial and underarm waxing, and makeup application ($950) for events. The full list is below.
