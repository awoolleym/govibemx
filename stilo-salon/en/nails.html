Nails · Roma Norte

Nails, manicure and pedicure in Roma Norte

From a simple polish to sculpted gel nails. The whole list with prices, so you can choose without asking.

Our nail work splits into three blocks: manicure and pedicure, gel and polish, and acrylic and sculpted nails. The difference between them is mostly how long the result lasts and how it treats your natural nail.

The spa manicure ($220) includes a salt soak, exfoliation, massage and polish — it is the full care service, not just color. If you want it to last longer, the spa manicure + gel ($350) swaps polish for gel, which holds two to three weeks without chipping. The spa pedicure ($360) adds heel filing and a foot massage.

If you are doing hands and feet the same day, the spa mani + spa pedi package with gel ($750) is the convenient option: both full services with gel on each.

In gel and polish we charge separately when you only want color: gel on hands ($180), gel on feet ($220) or regular polish ($150). All include up to two solid shades; additional shades or designs carry a small extra cost that we tell you beforehand.

Nail vitamins ($150) are treatments for the natural nail, not color. Calcium strengthens, rubber base covers imperfections and adds body to thin or weak nails, and nail vitamin protects while the nail recovers. If your nails are worn down after a long stretch of acrylic, start here.

In acrylic and sculpted nails we offer acrylic over the natural nail ($400), sculpted gel nails (from $500) and gel tips (from $450). Fills cost less than a full set, and we recommend them every three or four weeks — waiting longer damages the natural nail. Removal ($100) is always done properly, never by prying.

One honest note: not every hand needs acrylic. If your natural nail is in good shape, a well-applied gel looks just as good and treats it better. We will tell you so.

Prices in Mexican pesos (MXN).
