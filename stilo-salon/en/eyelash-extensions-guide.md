# The eyelash extension and brow bible

Want to know what they cost? Which techniques exist? Which suits you? How long they last? It is all here, written by the people who apply them.

## The eyelash and brow bible

Everything people ask us in the chair, written down. First things first: which one is which.

### The five techniques

#### Classic 1x1 — $750

One extension on each of your natural lashes. It is a full set, so how dense it looks depends on how much natural lash you have. This is the entry technique and the most natural. If you have never worn extensions, start here.

Application: 1 hora 30 minutos · Fill: $450

#### Flat — $800

Uses a flat-based fiber that wraps your natural lash instead of resting on a single point. It weighs less and bonds better, so it works well if your lashes are fine or brittle.

Application: 1 hora 30 minutos · Fill: $500

#### YY — $1,000

Y-shaped interlaced fibers: each extension covers twice the area without twice the weight. It creates a denser look without loading the natural lash. A good middle ground between classic and volume.

Application: 1 hora 30 minutos · Fill: $550

#### Hybrid — $1,100

Combines 1x1 and volume: one classic extension and one volume fan, alternating. Looks fuller even without much natural lash. It is the middle ground, and the set we are asked for most.

Application: 2 horas · Fill: $550

#### Russian volume — $1,200

Three extensions in a fan on each of your natural lashes. We do not recommend it if you have not worn hybrid or classic first — the natural lash needs to be prepared for the weight.

Application: 2 horas · Fill: $650

## Why fills are necessary

Your lashes follow a growth cycle: you shed roughly four or five a day, and each one takes its extension with it. That is why a fill is not an extra, it is part of the service. Extensions do not damage or pull out your natural lashes — what you see falling is the normal cycle.

The fill is priced at the lash artist's assessment, based on your aftercare, your growth and how much lash you still have: you need at least 50% retention. Past 21 days it is no longer a fill — most extensions will have shed — so it is charged as removal ($150) plus a new set.

## Caring for your extensions

- Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours.
- Do not use oil-based removers; micellar water is best.
- No steam and no sauna.
- Brush and wash daily — let water run over your lashes at least once a day.
- Do not use mascara.
- Do not rub your eyes hard.
- Book a fill every two to three weeks.

How long your extensions last depends directly on how you care for them. Well cared for and filled on schedule, they last as long as you want them to.

## Lash lift — $450

A lash lift raises your natural lash from the root for a natural effect. No synthetic hair is used: the process uses pigment and keratin, so it does not damage the lash. It includes black tint and lasts six to eight weeks, depending on the person and their aftercare. Application takes an hour and you can repeat it as often as you like.

- Keep dry for the first 24 hours.
- No steam and no sauna.
- No baby oil — almond oil or micellar water only.
- You can wear mascara after 24 hours.
- Avoid very hot water for the first three days.
